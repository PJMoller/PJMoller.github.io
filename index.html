02 A bit about me

I'm a second-year Technical Informatica student at NHL Stenden, and most of what I enjoy building lives in the gap between software and hardware — the part where code stops being abstract and starts turning motors, pumping liquid, or looking at a chessboard through a camera.

Along the way I've picked up Python, C, C++, Java, SQL, R and HTML, plus a working sense of how to run a project without it falling apart — some SCRUM, some Agile, some just keeping a to-do list that actually gets followed.

Before this I trained as an IT systems and devices technician at Alfa-college, and picked up two internships — at Multicast in Emmen and Kantor in Appingedam — that gave me a first real look at how this stuff works outside a classroom.
