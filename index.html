I'm a second-year Computer Science student at NHL Stenden. Technically the program is called "Technische Informatica", which doesn't translate directly, but it's closest to Computer Science or Computer Engineering. Most of what I like building sits between software and hardware, the point where code stops being abstract and starts turning a motor, pumping liquid, or reading a chessboard through a camera.

Along the way I've picked up Python, C, C++, Java, SQL, R and HTML, plus a decent sense of how to keep a project from falling apart. Some SCRUM, some Agile, but mostly just keeping a to-do list I actually follow.

Before NHL Stenden I trained as an IT systems and devices technician at Alfa-college, where I also did two internships as part of the program: one at Multicast in Emmen and one at Kantor in Appingedam. That was my first real look at how this stuff works outside a classroom.
